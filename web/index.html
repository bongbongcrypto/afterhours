A stock price that keeps answering when the exchange is closed.

AfterHours passes Chainlink's price through while the market is open. When the feed goes quiet, it answers with the Robinhood Chain pool's price, held within 1% of the last print during the US regular session, when a quiet feed means a quiet market, and within 10% overnight and at weekends. It refuses when the pool is too thin to trust. Every price, age and pool figure on this page is read from Robinhood Chain by your browser.

Why it exists

Measured on Robinhood Chain mainnet with the read-only scripts in the repository.

  • 52.2 h

    The AAPL/USD feed went without a print over an ordinary weekend scripts/measure/feed_cadence.py, and 76.2 h over Labor Day scripts/measure/weekend_swaps.py

  • $4.2M

    AAPL changed hands in the chain's Uniswap pools over the 09-11 weekend, in 26,331 swaps, at prices that moved; over the four September weekends, from $5.1M down to $0.58M. scripts/measure/weekend_swaps.py

  • $732k

    Borrowed against $756k supplied in the 81 funded Morpho markets that take these stock tokens as collateral (2026-09-30). $300k of it was borrowed in one transaction on Sunday 09-27, while AAPL's feed had been silent since Friday. Their oracles keep Friday's price through the weekend or read a raw pool price. scripts/measure/morpho_markets.py --borrows-since 2026-09-16

Use it

One contract per stock, with the Chainlink feed's interface and Morpho Blue's. A contract that reads the feed swaps one address; a Morpho market, which cannot change its oracle, is opened with AfterHours as the oracle.

A Morpho Blue market

AfterHours is the market's oracle. price() is Morpho's own interface; USDG stays the loan token.

MarketParams({
  loanToken:       USDG,
  collateralToken: AAPL,
  oracle:          afterHoursAAPL,
  irm:             AdaptiveCurveIrm,
  lltv:            0.625e18
});

Any Chainlink consumer

Swap the feed address. Round ids, decimals and description are the feed's; a refusal reverts instead of returning a stale price.

(, int256 answer,, uint256 updatedAt,) =
  AggregatorV3Interface(afterHoursAAPL)
    .latestRoundData();
// fresh in LIVE_FEED and ONCHAIN_TWAP;
// reverts IssuerPaused / NoData(reason)
